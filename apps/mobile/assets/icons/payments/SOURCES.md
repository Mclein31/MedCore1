Wallet icons render at 28px, QR Ph at 56×28 with its original wide proportions. Visa/Mastercard appear in card-network selection at 64×32 with labels. No logo shape/text was redrawn, no artwork was recolored, and no logo is placed inside the decodable QR symbol. Artwork identifies existing prototype rails; it does not add a provider integration or change payment destinations.

Bank assets were acquired from the official publishers on October 8, 2026 using Safari downloads. Both retain their full square artwork, including the publisher's background and lettering, with no crop or stretching. The bank selection interface must use contain sizing. These identifiers do not connect to banking services or request banking credentials.
