The care is done. Why is the bill still unclear? Meet MedCore: financial clarity for families, and a discharge target under forty-five minutes, not eight hours.

Before MedCore, there was a family trying to understand a bill.

A senior citizen, covered by PhilHealth for thirty-nine years, also had a private HMO.

The quoted bill: thirty-six thousand five hundred pesos.

The unexpected personal share: eight thousand three hundred. Coverage existed. Clarity was missing.

A later review brought the balance down to one thousand three hundred.

Why did understanding the bill take extra help? That question became the reason to build MedCore.

MedCore brings hospital charges, eligible benefits, and approved insurance coverage into one explainable ledger. A financial coordination layer alongside existing hospital systems. Guide. Verify. Reward. Report. Improve. One clearer picture for staff, and for the patient’s phone.

Guide starts with an estimate. Staff can explain expected costs and room choices early.

Verify brings coverage and authorization records into one worklist, making pending approvals and exclusions easier to review.

The reconciled ledger shows how charges, eligible adjustments, PhilHealth, and approved HMO coverage arrive at the patient’s responsibility.

On mobile, that picture becomes easier to follow: the remaining balance, an itemized bill, and the benefits already recorded.

Cora offers English, Taglish, Tagalog, and Ilokano language options. The important question stays simple: what do I owe, and why?

When the final bill is ready, the next step is clear. The prototype includes GCash, Maya, QR Ph, card, and bank options.

Review the amount, choose a method, and confirm.

Payments in this demonstration are simulated. The result is recorded with a receipt and an updated balance.

For eligible accounts, an approved zero-interest arrangement offers another supported path.

Documents keep the statement of account close at hand. Discharge passes require the recorded clearances.

Staff can follow the clearance stages and see what still needs attention.

ClaimReady supports pre-flight review of the electronic statement of account before submission.

Report makes bottlenecks visible. Improve helps teams examine recurring issues.

Clearer bills. Better coordination. Fewer avoidable delays.
