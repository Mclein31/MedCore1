# V4 cast and character art

Fictional characters that act out the founder-reported family experience in S1, S2 and S14. They are not the team, a real patient, or an identifiable person. The characters never speak; one narrator (Mara's point of view) tells the story.

Style: premium editorial 2D illustration with restrained 2.5D depth. Navy, slate, warm cream and muted teal, plus **one warm red accent reserved for pain moments**. Reuse `reference.png` as the model sheet for every new image (same outfits, hair and proportions).

| Character | Who | Look (locked) | Arc in V4 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tatay Ramon** (68) | Senior citizen; PhilHealth member for 39 years; also has a private HMO | Short silver hair, pale-blue polo, charcoal trousers | Ready to go home at 8 AM → still on the bed at 4 PM → walks out in daylight (S14) |
| **Mara** (35) | His daughter and guarantor; narrator's voice | Shoulder-length dark hair, dark-teal blouse, warm cardigan, navy trousers, plain folder | Asks, waits, asks again → relieved but tired → confident, with the exit pass on her phone |
| **Lina** (31) | Hospital billing officer | Neat dark hair, sage top, plain lanyard with no readable ID | Not the villain: buried in portals, calls and calculators. Her pain is staff pain, and MedCore helps her too |

Change from V3: the characters are now named on screen ("Tatay Ramon"). Mara becomes the narrator's perspective, which makes S1 personal. Lina is reframed as the second victim of the broken process, so the film shows staff pain as well as patient pain.
