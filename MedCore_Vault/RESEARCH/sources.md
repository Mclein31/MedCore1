## 2. PhilHealth Official Circulars & Advisories

1. [FACT] **PhilHealth Circular No. 2019-0002**  
   - *Title*: "Guidelines on the Electronic Submission of Claim Form 4 (CF4) for All Case Rates Claims"  
   - *Issuing Body*: Philippine Health Insurance Corporation  
   - *Cited Mandates*: Standardized electronic clinical abstract, mandatory vital signs, physical exam findings, course in the ward, and MAR logs.

2. [FACT] **PhilHealth Circular No. 2023-0026**  
   - *Title*: "Mandatory Electronic Submission of Statement of Account (eSOA) in Standardized XML Format for Accredited Healthcare Institutions"  
   - *Issuing Body*: Philippine Health Insurance Corporation  
   - *Cited Mandates*: Deprecation of scanned PDF/image SOAs; mandatory XML schema validation against the PhilHealth Standard Library.

3. [FACT] **PhilHealth Advisory No. 2026-eClaims 3.0**  
   - *Title*: "Technical Specifications and Implementation Rules for eClaims Version 3.0 Gateway"  
   - *Issuing Body*: PhilHealth Information Technology Management Department (ITMD)  
   - *Cited Mandates*: Automated XML validation, cryptographic checksums, and strict rejection protocols for mismatched charges.

4. [FACT] **PhilHealth Board Resolution & Press Advisory (October 1, 2024)**  
   - *Title*: "Official Suspension of the Single Period of Confinement (SPC) Policy for Repeated Inpatient Admissions"  
   - *Issuing Body*: PhilHealth Corporate Communications / Office of the President  
   - *Cited Action*: Immediate suspension of the 90-day same-illness restriction across all accredited healthcare institutions.
